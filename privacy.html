Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

What stays on your device

Your plants, care history, and watering reminders live on your phone. They leave it only when you choose to back up your garden or open your garden gate, both described below.

What we store for your gardener

An anonymous account id and a generated gardener name. If you link an email, that address, used only to send you sign-in codes; it is never shown to other gardeners. Anything you choose to post to the Help Garden (photo, plant facts, your words). Who you follow, who you block, and reports you file. A push notification token so reply notifications can reach you. Two anonymous usage events (app opened, plant added) so we can tell whether the app is helping people.

Garden backups

When you back up your garden, we store a copy of your plants, their nicknames, care history, spirit bonds, outfits, and layout, tied to your gardener. The recovery code that can restore it is kept only as a one-way hash, so we cannot read your code back or recover it for you. While you keep using the app, the copy refreshes about once a day.

Visiting gardens

If you open your garden gate, a snapshot of your island is shared with signed-in gardeners: your plants' names and species, how each plant is doing, your spirits and their outfits, and your layout. Never your photos, care history, or email. Closing the gate deletes the snapshot.

Plant identification

Photos you send for plant identification are forwarded to the Pl@ntNet identification service and are not stored by us. When the app cannot match a result to its catalog, it keeps the suggested plant name (never the photo) so we know which care sheets to add next.

What we never do

No selling data, no advertising trackers, no third-party analytics.

Deleting your data

Deleting a post removes it permanently. Deleting your community account (from your profile) removes your identity, your linked email, posts, photos, follows, blocks, reports, push token, garden backups, and your garden snapshot. Your plants stay on your phone.
