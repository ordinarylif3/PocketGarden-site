Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026

What stays on your device

Your plants, care history, and watering reminders live only on your phone. They are never uploaded.

What we store

An anonymous account id and a generated gardener name. Anything you choose to post to the Help Garden (photo, plant facts, your words). Who you follow and block. A push notification token so reply notifications can reach you. Two anonymous usage events (app opened, plant added) so we can tell whether the app is helping people.

What we never do

No selling data, no advertising trackers, no third-party analytics. Photos you send for plant identification are forwarded to the Pl@ntNet identification service and are not stored by us.

Deleting your data

Deleting a post removes it permanently. Deleting your community account (from your profile) removes your identity, posts, photos, follows, and push token. Your plants stay on your phone.
