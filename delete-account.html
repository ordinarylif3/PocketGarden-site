Delete your account

Last updated: August 20, 2026

Pocket Garden creates an anonymous gardener identity so you can ask and answer questions in the Help Garden, and lets you link an email to it if you choose. This page explains how to delete that identity and everything attached to it. Google Play requires this page to be reachable from the web, so you can read it even after uninstalling the app.

Delete from inside the app

Open Pocket Garden, tap Help Garden, open your own profile with the My profile button in the top right, then scroll to Your account and choose Delete community account. You will be asked to confirm twice, because it cannot be undone.

What deletion removes

Your anonymous account id and gardener name, your linked email if you added one, every help thread you posted and its photo, every reply you wrote, who you follow, who you have blocked, reports you filed, your push notification token, any garden backups, and your garden snapshot if your gate was open. Removal is immediate and permanent; there is no recovery window.

What stays on your phone

Your plants, their nicknames, and their whole care history live on your device, so deleting your community account does not touch them. Uninstalling the app removes them, and that cannot be undone unless you made a backup first.

If you already uninstalled the app

If you linked an email, reinstall the app, choose Restore, then Linked email, and sign in with the code we send you; you can then delete the account from your profile as described above. If you never linked an email, the identity cannot be signed back into: email the address below and tell us the gardener name you posted under so we can find and remove it.
