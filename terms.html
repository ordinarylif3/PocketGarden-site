Terms of Service

Last updated: August 20, 2026

Pocket Garden helps you care for real plants and share plant questions with other gardeners. By using the app you agree to these terms.

Your account

The app creates an anonymous gardener identity for you: no password, no real name, and no email unless you choose to link one. Linking an email from your profile lets you sign back in as the same gardener on another phone. It is optional, and the app works fully without it. You can delete your identity at any time from your profile; deletion removes your community posts permanently.

Community rules

The Help Garden is for plant questions and answers. Do not post spam, harassment, hateful content, or anything unrelated to plants. Content can be reported by anyone; repeatedly reported content is hidden automatically pending review, and we may remove content or accounts that break these rules.

Your content

You own what you post. By posting to the Help Garden you let us show it to other users of the app. Deleting a thread removes it and its photo permanently. If you open your garden gate, a snapshot of your island is shown to other gardeners in the same way until you close it.

Garden backups

If you back up your garden, a copy of your plants and their care history is stored on our servers so it can be restored on another phone, using your recovery code or your linked email. Keep the recovery code private: anyone who has it can restore a copy of your garden.

Plant care advice

Care reminders and community answers are guidance from data and fellow hobbyists, not professional horticultural advice. Plants are wonderfully unpredictable.

Changes

If these terms change in a way that matters, the app will say so before the change applies.
